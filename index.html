Technology that works quietly in the background.

Subtlmind is an engineering company that brings together IoT sensors, deep learning and language models. We believe the best technology works quietly in the background: it warns you before something breaks and handles routine adjustments, so your team doesn't have to watch screens all day.

We collect data from your equipment, cameras and building systems and turn it into clear alerts, answers and automatic actions. We work mainly with factories, warehouses, commercial buildings and farms, and also on premium residential projects.
