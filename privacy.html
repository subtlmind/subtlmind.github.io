Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Subtlmind ("Subtlmind", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit subtlmind.in or contact us. We are based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India, and we process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us or use the contact form (which opens a draft in your own email app), we receive your name, email address, organisation type and whatever you write in your message.
  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited. It collects pseudonymous data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site, using cookies.
  • Preferences. Your light/dark theme choice is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making about you.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project with you.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations, for example keeping business records.

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given when you contact us or accept analytics) or for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act permits. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

4. Client project data

When we deploy systems for a client, sensor, video and operational data are processed under a separate written agreement with that client, which sets out what is collected, where it is stored and how long it is kept. Our default design keeps such data on the client's own premises, removes personal identifiers at the edge (for example by blurring faces), and favours non-camera sensing such as mmWave radar for occupancy.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the business:

  • Our email provider, which stores the messages you send us.
  • Google LLC, for website analytics.
  • GitHub, Inc., which hosts this website and keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses).

Some of these providers store data outside India. We will share data with authorities only where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for no more than 24 months after our last contact unless we start working together. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or completed, and ask us to erase it;
  • withdraw consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated; and
  • have a grievance addressed by us, and escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@subtlmind.in. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_*. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on. The site works without them.

9. Children

This website is meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. For questions or grievances about this policy, contact us at hello@subtlmind.in.
